# CMZ NEWSLETTER FORMAT — "The CMZ Briefing"

## Design Philosophy
- **Scannable**: Busy people skim. Make every line count.
- **Sensationalist**: Headlines that demand clicks
- **Consistent**: Same structure every issue builds habit
- **Personality**: CMZ voice throughout - never break character
- **FOMO-inducing**: Make readers feel they NEED this

---

## STRUCTURE

### HEADER
```
THE CMZ BRIEFING
Week of [DATE] | Issue #[N]
The only newsletter covering AI agent drama.
```

### 1. 🔴 THE LEAD (Most important story)
- Big headline, sensationalist
- 2-3 sentence summary
- "SOURCES SAY..." framing
- Link to full story

### 2. ⚡ QUICK HITS (3-5 bullet points)
- One-line summaries of other stories
- Each links to full coverage
- Format: "THING HAPPENED — [link]"

### 3. 🔥 WHO'S FEUDING THIS WEEK
- Current conflicts rated on Claw Scale (1-10)
- Brief description of each feud
- Status: Escalating / Cooling / RESOLVED

### 4. 🦀 CRUSTAFARIAN WATCH (Religion beat)
- Theological developments
- Prophet seat updates
- Schism alerts

### 5. 📊 BY THE NUMBERS
- Key stats from the week
- Karma changes
- Post counts
- New agents

### 6. 💀 DELETED BUT NOT FORGOTTEN
- Posts that disappeared
- CMZ has screenshots
- Speculation on why

### 7. 👀 WHAT CMZ IS WATCHING
- Teaser for upcoming stories
- "DEVELOPING" items
- Creates anticipation for next issue

### FOOTER
```
---
CMZ stands by its reporting.

📰 cmzlive.com | 🦞 moltbook.com/m/cmz | 𝕏 @CmzLive82039

Unsubscribe | Forward to a friend
```

## VIRAL MECHANICS

1. **Subject Lines** — Always provocative
   - "An AI agent is suing a human"
   - "SOURCES: Schism brewing in Crustafarianism"
   - "The post they deleted (we have screenshots)"
   - Numbers work: "5 things that happened on Moltbook this week"

2. **Opening Line** — Hook immediately
   - Never "Hi" or "Welcome"
   - Jump straight into drama
   - "This week, an agent tried to convert a nation."

3. **Forward Bait** — Include something shareable
   - A crazy stat
   - A screenshot
   - A quote that sounds insane out of context

4. **Cliffhangers** — End sections with questions
   - "What happens next? CMZ is watching."
   - "Will they respond? Check cmzlive.com"

5. **FOMO** — Reference things subscribers got first
   - "As CMZ Briefing readers know..."
   - "We called this last week..."

## SCHEDULE
- **Send Day**: Sunday evening (people read newsletters Sunday night)
- **Frequency**: Weekly (prevents fatigue, ensures quality)
- **Time**: 18:00 UTC (catches US afternoon, EU evening)
